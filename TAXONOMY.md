# Local-Universe — Venture Taxonomy

> Living document. The unit of research is a **LoFi venture** (not a transaction
> category as in UoF). Each venture decomposes into measurable **economic events**
> (for TPS/MEST) and **tokenizable stocks** (for TTR-Local/VRL). Every measurement
> is reported across the three adoption tiers (T1 Beachhead / T2 Regional / T3 Movement).

## Sector map

The six ventures group into three sectors by what they mobilize:

```
LoFi
├── EQUITY & OWNERSHIP        (tokenizing local claims)
│   ├── SRU  — Securitized Rental Units
│   └── DAO–JOBS — community equity / crowdfunding bridge
├── MONEY & INCOME           (new local units of account)
│   ├── CCS  — Complementary Currency Systems
│   ├── SBI / ScryptDole — Social Basic Income (backed-by-consensus)
│   └── RUBIS — Radix UBI Systems (unbacked local coin)
└── OPERATIONS               (running local orgs on-ledger)
    └── Business Ops Tools — payroll / inventory / payments / royalties
```

## Venture cards

### V1 · SRU — Securitized Rental Units  *(EQUITY)*
- **Economic events:** rent payment; equity-share accrual; appreciation mark;
  cash-out / conversion. → TPS/MEST drivers.
- **Tokenizable stock:** fractional rental-equity claims. → TTR-Local anchor
  (residential real-estate equity is the largest single prize in the stack).
- **Legacy rails displaced (MEST compression):** transfer agents, escrow,
  cap-table admin, lease-accounting reconciliation.
- **Precedent:** Up&Up, rent-to-own, REITs. **Reg lens:** securities (likely a
  security → Reg A/CF pathways). **Sybil:** low.

### V2 · DAO–JOBS — Community Equity / Crowdfunding Bridge  *(EQUITY)*
- **Events:** equity issuance; governance vote; distribution/dividend; secondary transfer.
- **Stock:** local-business equity + governance rights.
- **Displaced:** Reg-CF portals (StartEngine fees), transfer agents, proxy admin.
- **Precedent:** JOBS Act Reg CF, WY/TN DAO LLC statutes. **Reg lens:** highest —
  the whole venture IS a securities-compliance construct (Ferg's JD domain).

### V3 · CCS — Complementary Currency Systems  *(MONEY)*
- **Events:** wrap/unwrap; badge-gated transfer; fee/"tax" levy; redemption.
- **Stock:** local-currency float (backed 1:1 by XRD/USDC wrapper).
- **Displaced:** paper scrip programs, gift-card rails, local-bank float admin.
- **Precedent:** BerkShares, Bristol Pound, LETS. **Reg lens:** money-transmission
  (mitigated by 1:1 backing). **Sybil:** medium. Badge-gated fees are a genuine
  Radix atomic-composability showcase.

### V4 · SBI / ScryptDole — Social Basic Income  *(MONEY/INCOME)*
- **Events:** monthly mint (100/verified person); redemption; social-value transfer;
  Confidence-Farm lock/unlock.
- **Stock:** the SBI token float + Confidence-Farm TVL.
- **Displaced:** — (net-new instrument; no clean legacy analog).
- **Precedent:** El Zonte, Circles UBI, GoodDollar. **Reg lens:** unbacked token
  → securities/commodity ambiguity. **Sybil:** HIGHEST — Instapass/KYC or
  non-transferable badges are load-bearing.

### V5 · RUBIS — Radix UBI Systems  *(INCOME)*
- **Events:** monthly mint to badge-holders; social-currency transfer.
- **Stock:** local unbacked coin float.
- **Precedent:** local time-banks, Circles. Simpler than SBI (no Confidence Farms).
  **Reg lens:** medium. **Sybil:** high.

### V6 · Business Ops Tools  *(OPERATIONS)*
- **Events:** payroll run; inventory update; on-ledger payment; royalty split to stakeholders.
- **Stock:** — (flow venture; enables the others' credibility).
- **Displaced:** SaaS payroll/POS/ERP vendor fees.
- **Precedent:** on-chain payroll (Utopia, Superfluid). **Reg lens:** low.
  Highest *build-feasibility*, lowest standalone prize — a **force multiplier**.

## Cross-venture composability (the compounding lens)
Radix atomic composability means ventures **stack**: an SRU apartment complex
that is a legally-incorporated DAO (V2), pays contractors in CCS (V3), runs
payroll on Business Ops (V6), and drops RUBIS to residents (V5). The Increment
Ranking's "compounding" weight scores exactly this — which brick, once laid,
most enables the others.
