# The Adoption-Tier Model (foundational — reused by all ventures)

> Model: **communities × people/community × $ addressable local activity**, with a
> penetration haircut per tier. Each tier is anchored to a real analog and discounted
> for the optimism bias endemic to crypto-adoption stories. El Zonte is the base rate:
> only ~36% of surveyed households actively used Bitcoin even after years of promotion.
> Souls: Market-Sizing Specialist × Community-Development Economist. Accessed 2026-07-23.

## T1 — Beachhead (1 flagship town)
| Parameter | Value | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Population | 3,000–10,000 (El Zonte ≈ 3,000) | [El Zonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Zonte) |
| Active-household penetration | 20–40% (El Zonte 36% of 192 surveyed) | [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0016718524000411) |
| $ addressable local base | $15M–$75M/yr 🔴 (5k × $3k–15k/capita local spend) | derived |

Value here is proof-of-concept + circular-economy template, not raw dollars.

## T2 — Regional (100–1,000 communities)
| Parameter | Value | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Community count | 100–1,000 | Transition Towns ~2,000–3,000 initiatives / 50–67 countries ([Transition Network](https://transitionnetwork.org/about/)) |
| People reached | 0.5M–5M | derived |
| $ addressable base | $2.5B–$25B/yr 🔴 | derived |

Ceiling read: BerkShares moved >$10M cumulatively over ~18yr (realistically <$1M/yr net) —
these networks are **wide but shallow in dollar throughput**. Model T2 as reachable *communities*.

## T3 — Movement (~1% of global local economies)
| Parameter | Value | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Universe | ~90k US local govts; ~160k in Council of Europe; **~400M MSMEs (~50% of global GDP)** | [St. Louis Fed](https://www.stlouisfed.org/publications/regional-economist/2024/march/local-governments-us-number-type) · [UN MSME](https://www.un.org/en/observances/micro-small-medium-businesses-day) |
| 1% slice — communities | ~2,000–5,000 municipalities 🔴 | derived |
| 1% slice — economic base | Global GDP ~$110T × ~50% MSME = ~$55T local output; **1% ≈ ~$550B/yr** | derived |

The honest global "local economy" denominator is fuzzy; the **MSME lens (400M firms, ~50% of GDP)
is the defensible substitute**. 1% movement-scale ≈ **~$550B/yr addressable** — the top-of-funnel
prize, which VRL then discounts to what's actually recapturable.
