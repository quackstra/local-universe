## Headline
SRU's prize is dominated by **TTR-Local** — a tokenizable rental-equity base from
~$3M (one town) to **~$45B–$4.0T at movement scale**, whose aggressive ceiling lands
exactly on Deloitte's independent **$4T-tokenized-real-estate-by-2035** forecast. Throughput
and Radix capture are real but secondary (T3 ≈ 6–15 net-new TPS, 7.5–10M net-new accounts —
transformational vs Radix's ~7M-tx lifetime baseline; up to ~$48M/yr builder royalties). Honest
caveat: every T2/T3 figure rides on two explicit unsourced assumptions — SRU penetration of the
rental base and the equity-fraction actually tokenized — so the **defensible near-term story is
the 3–10% renter-wallet floor (Up&Up-proven)**, with the trillion-dollar ceiling reserved for a
full-securitization, decade-horizon maturity.
