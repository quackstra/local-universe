# CCS — Complementary Currency Systems · Prize Capsule (Run 1)

> A local token backed 1:1 by a wrapper (XRD/USDC), with badge-gated transfer fees
> that keep value circulating locally; reusable Radix blueprints.
> Souls: Complementary/Alternative-Currency Economist × CBDC/Digital-Currency Researcher.

**Assumptions:** velocity 4–12 turns/yr · avg tx $10–25 · float/capita $50–300 · 1.25× event multiplier.

## Headline
CCS scales from a rounding-error beachhead (T1 ~$0.05–1.8M float, sub-0.1 TPS) to a genuinely
large movement (T3 **$3–15B tokenized TVL, up to ~700 net-new TPS, $1.8–81B/yr retained locally**).
The most persuasive lens is **VRL** — it monetizes the entire *raison d'être* of a local currency
on robust local-multiplier evidence ($45 vs $14 per $100, AMIBA), and it is the only lens whose
value grows *because* Radix's badge-gated fee makes leakage costly. Honest caveat: **every historical
local currency in the anchor set shrank or died** — Bristol Pound (£1M peak → wound down 2020 on
legacy-rail maintenance cost + thin volume), Ithaca HOURS (defunct), BerkShares (plateaued ~$133k
after 20 years) — so the durable win is not "issue a town token" but the **reusable-blueprint +
~7→1 MEST compression** that removes the exact operational overhead that killed the predecessors.
