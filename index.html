The question

What is the "size of prize," and which increment wins?

Two 2022 concept papers — ScryptDole (unbacked social basic income) and The Local Stack (a menu of local-DeFi "LoFi" blueprints) — describe six ventures for empowering local economies on Radix. This study sizes each in the language the Radix community optimizes for, then ranks which one returns the most impact per unit of build effort. Every number is a counterfactual range, sourced, reported across three adoption tiers.

T1 · BEACHHEAD

1 flagship town, ~3–10k people. Anchor: El Zonte ("Bitcoin Beach") — a sobering base rate: only ~36% of households actively transacted after years.

T2 · REGIONAL

100–1,000 communities. Anchor: BerkShares / Transition-Towns network scale — wide reach, but historically shallow dollar throughput.

T3 · MOVEMENT

~1% of global local economies. Anchor: 400M MSMEs ≈ 50% of global GDP → ~$550B/yr addressable local base.

MEST — the "why Radix specifically" lens. At T3 the stack captures ~45–70B new on-ledger state-transitions/yr and compresses ~300–470B legacy transitions/yr (~86%, 7→1) — but only for the four ventures with real incumbent rails (SRU, CCS, DAO-JOBS, Business Ops). The 7:1 constant does not survive contact with SBI/RUBIS: unbacked social-income tokens have no legacy rail to compress, so their honest ratio is ~1.2–1.5:1. The composability dividend is huge where there's an incumbent to displace, near-zero where the instrument is net-new.

Build one thing → CCS

Best balance of prize, buildability and regulatory safety, and the single cleanest "only-possible-on-Radix" demo: badge-gated fees + atomic wrap/unwrap. Real precedent (BerkShares), 1:1 backed, strong sourced VRL story.

Build a sequence → Business Ops → CCS → SRU

Lay the rails first (Business Ops: top compounding + feasibility), ship the flagship showcase second (CCS), fast-follow with the hero/biggest-prize venture (SRU) once securities pathway + rails are proven. Gate SBI/RUBIS behind solved proof-of-personhood — don't lead with them.

Biggest data gap. A primary-source field study of local spend composition + realistic chain→local shift in one candidate beachhead town. VRL — the lens carrying the community case — swings ~30× on two unsourced inputs. The highest-ROI research increment is a single-town study; it de-risks the T1 numbers for every venture at once.
The gravity

The honest floor

Every T2/T3 figure rides on adoption the study cannot yet source. Two forces pull hard against the ceilings — and the durable Radix win is engineering out exactly what killed the predecessors.

The El Zonte base rate

Even the flagship crypto-town saw only ~36% of households actively transacting after years of promotion. Assume 1-in-3, not universal uptake.

The complementary-currency graveyard

Bristol Pound wound down (2020), Ithaca HOURS defunct, BerkShares plateaued at ~$133k after 20 years — killed by legacy-rail maintenance cost.

The actual moat

Not "issue a town token" but the reusable-blueprint + ~7→1 MEST compression that removes the operational overhead the graveyard died of.
