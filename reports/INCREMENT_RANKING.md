## Reading the result

The top three are **tightly bunched (69.8 / 68.5 / 65.8)** and represent three *different
strategies*, not three grades of the same one:

- **CCS wins on balance** — the best Radix-fit of the six (badge-gated fees + atomic wrap/unwrap
  is the single cleanest showcase of what Radix uniquely does), a strong sourced VRL narrative,
  manageable money-transmission risk (1:1 backed), and real precedent (BerkShares). It is the best
  *standalone* first build.
- **SRU carries the biggest prize** (90) — a $45B–$4T tokenizable-equity base that lands on
  Deloitte's independent $4T-by-2035 forecast — but pays for it with the heaviest build and
  securities exposure. It's the *hero* venture, not the *opener*.
- **Business Ops is the force-multiplier** — modest standalone prize (50) but the top compounding
  score (90) and best feasibility (75): it's the payments/payroll rail every other venture rides on,
  and the dominant XRD sink (~$400M–1B/yr burned at T3).

SBI and RUBIS sit at the bottom **not for lack of ambition but for lack of a floor**: unbacked value
can collapse toward zero (Circles-Berlin 90% cash-out), Sybil-resistance is unsolved-and-expensive,
and regulatory exposure is the worst of the six. Their one genuine strength — fastest account
acquisition in crypto — is an RVC story, not a value story.

## Recommendation

**Two honest answers, depending on the question:**

1. **Build ONE thing for maximum standalone ROI → CCS.** Best balance of prize, buildability,
   regulatory safety, and it's the sharpest "only-possible-on-Radix" demo to show the community.

2. **Build a platform SEQUENCE → Business Ops → CCS → SRU.** Lay the rails first (Business Ops:
   highest compounding + feasibility), ship the flagship consumer showcase second (CCS: best
   Radix-fit + VRL story), then fast-follow with the hero venture (SRU: biggest prize) once the
   securities pathway and rails are proven. Gate SBI/RUBIS behind a solved proof-of-personhood — do
   not lead with them.

My read: **Ferg's original "SRU-leads" prior is half-right** — SRU has the biggest prize but not the
best ROI once feasibility and regulation are weighted. The two purpose-built-for-Radix ventures
(CCS, SRU) lead, and they trade off: **SRU = prize, CCS = buildability.** If forced to a single
answer, I'd open with **CCS** and treat SRU as the immediate fast-follow.

## Biggest data gap (blocks confidence upgrades)

A primary-source measurement of **local spend composition + realistic chain→local shift in one
candidate beachhead town.** VRL — the lens that carries the community/net-impact case — swings ~30×
on two unsourced inputs (per-capita addressable spend, recapture fraction). The highest-ROI *research*
increment is a single-town field study; it de-risks the T1 numbers for every venture at once.
