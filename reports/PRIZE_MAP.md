> thesis, expressed as *net-new Radix-native activity* across five lenses at three
> adoption tiers. Every figure is a counterfactual range from Runs 1–2 (2026-07-23),

> deliberate — under this much adoption uncertainty a defensible band beats a false point.

## MEST — the "why Radix specifically" lens

Two-sided, per the framework: LoFi both **captures** new state-transitions on-ledger and
**compresses** the ones legacy rails fan out.

- **Captured (new on-ledger MEST/yr, T3):** ~45–70 billion, led by Business Ops (~40B) and CCS (0.6–22.5B).
- **Compressed (legacy MEST displaced/yr, T3):** ~**300–470 billion**, an **~86% (7→1) reduction** — but *only* for the four ventures with real legacy rails to compress (SRU, CCS, DAO-JOBS, Business Ops). **The 7:1 constant does not survive contact with SBI/RUBIS** — unbacked social-income tokens have no incumbent rail, so their honest compression is ~1.2–1.5:1. This is the study's most important methodological finding: the composability dividend is huge where there's an incumbent to displace, and near-zero where the instrument is net-new.

## Double-counting discipline

VRL is scoped per venture to limit overlap: **DAO-JOBS** = ownership/capital-return leakage,
**Business Ops** = transaction-fee leakage, **CCS** = circulation retention. Residual overlap
remains (a single dollar can be retained by more than one mechanism), so the aggregate VRL is a
**ceiling**, not a sum to bank. Account counts overlap heavily (one resident uses several ventures)
and are therefore reported as a range, never summed.

## The honest floor

Every T2/T3 figure rides on adoption assumptions the study cannot yet source. The **El Zonte base
rate** (only ~36% of households actively transacting after years) and the **complementary-currency
graveyard** (Bristol Pound wound down, Ithaca HOURS defunct, BerkShares plateaued at ~$133k) are the
gravity. The defensible near-term story is the **T1 beachhead** — a single town — with the
trillion-dollar ceilings reserved for a decade-horizon, sharding-era movement.
